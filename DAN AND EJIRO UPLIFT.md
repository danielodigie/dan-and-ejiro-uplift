

# **DAN AND EJIRO UPLIFT**

## **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

**Product:** Dan and Ejiro Uplift  
**Product Type:** Everyday Encouragement & Personal Growth Platform  
**Product Vision:** To help people maintain hope, strength, confidence, motivation, and purpose through meaningful encouragement delivered at the right moments.

---

## **1\. Product Overview**

**Dan and Ejiro Uplift** is an encouragement platform designed to support people throughout their everyday lives.

The product is built around a simple belief:

> **You don't have to be down to need encouragement.**

Uplift should support people who are struggling, but it should also strengthen people who are already doing well and encourage them to keep moving forward.

Instead of simply presenting random motivational quotes, Uplift should understand something about the person's current situation and provide encouragement that feels relevant, personal, positive, and meaningful.

The product should combine:

* Daily encouragement  
* Personalized messages  
* Timely encouragement  
* Motivation  
* Positive reflection  
* Goal support  
* Small actionable challenges  
* Optional faith-based encouragement  
* Community and human connection  
* Opportunities to pay for additional value

---

# **2\. Problem Statement**

People experience different emotional and life situations throughout the day.

Someone may wake up motivated but become discouraged after receiving bad news.

Someone may be working toward an important goal and need reassurance.

Someone may be successful but needs encouragement to maintain momentum.

Someone may simply need to hear:

> “Keep going. You're doing better than you think.”

Existing motivational content is often generic, repetitive, or disconnected from the user's actual circumstances.

**Dan and Ejiro Uplift aims to make encouragement more personal, timely, meaningful, and actionable.**

---

# **3\. Product Vision**

To become a trusted everyday source of encouragement that helps people:

* Keep going when life becomes difficult.  
* Believe in themselves.  
* Build confidence.  
* Maintain motivation.  
* Celebrate progress.  
* Recover from setbacks.  
* Strengthen their relationships.  
* Pursue meaningful goals.  
* Develop positive habits.

# **4\. Target Users**

Uplift will serve a broad audience, including:

### **Young Adults**

Students, graduates and young people navigating education, relationships, career and identity.

### **Professionals**

People dealing with work pressure, career decisions, business challenges and personal responsibilities.

### **People Experiencing Difficult Seasons**

People facing disappointment, failure, uncertainty, loneliness, financial pressure or major life changes.

### **People Pursuing Goals**

Users working toward personal, educational, financial, career, fitness, creative or entrepreneurial goals.

### **People Who Are Already Doing Well**

Users who want to maintain their confidence, gratitude, discipline and momentum.

### **Faith-Based Users**

Users who appreciate encouragement connected to God, Scripture, prayer and faith.

# **7\. Key Features**

## **Feature 1: Today's Uplift**

The home experience should provide a meaningful daily message.

Example:

> **Good morning, Daniel.**

> You don't need to solve everything today. Focus on the next step, do it well, and trust yourself enough to keep moving.

The message should feel personal rather than like a random quote.

### **User actions**

Users can:

* Save the message  
* Share it  
* Like it  
* Request another message  
* Reflect on it  
* Turn it into an action  
* Send it to someone else

---

# **8\. Feature 2: "I Need a Lift"**

This should be one of the most important features.

Whenever users need immediate encouragement, they can tap:

### **I Need a Lift**

They then choose what they need.

Examples:

* I feel discouraged  
* I need confidence  
* I'm overwhelmed  
* I need motivation  
* I experienced failure  
* I'm afraid to start  
* I feel lonely  
* I need hope  
* I need to keep going  
* I have an important decision  
* I need strength

Uplift responds with an appropriate message.

---

# **9\. Feature 3: Mood Check-In**

Users can regularly tell Uplift how they feel.

Instead of making this feel clinical, the experience should be simple and welcoming.

Example:

> **How are you feeling right now?**

😊 Great  
🙂 Good  
😐 Okay  
😔 Low  
😣 Struggling

The check-in can lead directly into personalized encouragement.

---

# **10\. Feature 4: Situation-Based Encouragement**

Users can identify what is happening in their lives.

Examples:

### **Career**

> "I'm struggling to find a job."

### **Business**

> "My business isn't growing."

### **Education**

> "I'm finding my studies difficult."

### **Relationships**

> "I'm dealing with relationship problems."

### **Finances**

> "Money is stressing me."

### **Personal Growth**

> "I want to become better."

The product should respond with encouragement relevant to the situation.

---

# **11\. Feature 5: Goal Uplift**

Users should be able to create personal goals.

For example:

**Goal:** Get a new job

Uplift can encourage the user along the journey.

Instead of only saying:

> "You can do it\!"

It might say:

> "You've already taken several steps toward your goal. Today, focus on one useful action: improve one part of your CV or apply for one opportunity."

The goal is to turn encouragement into **forward movement**.

---

# **12\. Feature 6: Uplift Actions**

Encouragement should sometimes lead to action.

After receiving a message, users may receive a small challenge.

Examples:

### **Confidence**

**Action:** Write down three things you're good at.

### **Career**

**Action:** Apply for one opportunity today.

### **Relationships**

**Action:** Send someone you appreciate a message.

### **Personal Growth**

**Action:** Spend 15 minutes working on something you've been postponing.

### **Gratitude**

**Action:** Write down three things you're grateful for.

The actions should feel achievable rather than overwhelming.

---

# **13\. Feature 7: Timely Uplifts**

Uplift should encourage users at meaningful moments throughout their day.

Potential moments include:

### **Morning**

"Start today with intention."

### **Midday**

"Take a breath. You're still moving forward."

### **Before an important event**

"You don't need perfection. Give yourself permission to begin."

### **Evening**

"Look at what you accomplished today, not only what remains."

Users should control how often they receive encouragement so the product never becomes annoying.

# **29\. Important Product Principle**

Uplift should **never make users feel dependent on the product for their emotional wellbeing**.

Its purpose should be to encourage people to:

* Take positive action  
* Connect with people  
* Pursue their goals  
* Practice gratitude  
* Build confidence  
* Seek appropriate support when needed  
* Develop their own resilience

Uplift should be a **companion**, not a replacement for human relationships or professional support.

The user taps:

**I'm ready.**

---

The user feels discouraged.

**I Need a Lift → Career → Discouraged**

**Today's small action:**

> Apply for one opportunity before the end of the day.

# **36\. Product Differentiation**

The central differentiation should not be:

> **"We have motivational quotes."**

Instead:

> **"We understand what you're facing and give you meaningful encouragement for the moment."**

The strongest product loop is:

### **Understand → Encourage → Act → Reflect → Grow → Return**

This gives Dan and Ejiro Uplift a purpose beyond content consumption.

# **38\. Product North Star**

Every major product decision should answer one question:

> **Does this help the user feel encouraged, strengthened, and more capable of taking their next positive step?**

If the answer is yes, it belongs within the spirit of **Dan and Ejiro Uplift**.

If it only creates engagement without creating meaningful value, it should be reconsidered.
