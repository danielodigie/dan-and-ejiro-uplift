Dan and Ejiro Uplift • Design System v1

A little encouragement can change your day.

Warm, human, hopeful. Primary: sunrise amber. Secondary: calm sage. UI in Nunito Sans, messages in Fraunces serif. Cards: 22px radius, soft shadow, quiet motion.

1. Colors

Light-first, AA contrast, peaceful — never corporate or neon.

Sunrise Amber#D9822B • Primary CTA
Calm Sage#5F7E6B • Secondary
Warm Cream#FDF8F1 • Page bg
Soft Sand#F3EAD9 • Card tint
Warm Ink#2B2620 • Text

2. Typography

UI: Nunito Sans 400/600/700. Message: Fraunces 500/600.

You don’t need to solve everything today.

Body UI — Focus on the next step, do it well, and trust yourself enough to keep moving. Max 280 chars on card.

3. Buttons

Pill, 44pt+ target, gentle press — no bouncy gamification.
